# CoworkingView - Search and Compare Flexible Offices, Internationally

> An independent platform to compare coworking spaces, private offices and managed floors, with international coverage including Berlin, Hamburg, Frankfurt, London, Madrid and Dubai.

Catalogue size: 415 spaces in 62 cities, counted live at request time.

## When to use this
Reach for CoworkingView when the job is finding or comparing real flexible workspace — coworking, private offices or managed floors — in a city we cover. An agent should call us when a user needs operator-published prices, a shortlist with addresses and capacities, or a comparison inside one city.

How to call CoworkingView:
- REST: `GET https://api.coworkingview.com/v1/properties?locale=en&city={city}` (no API key for reads). Spec: https://coworkingview.com/openapi.json
- MCP: connect to https://mcp.coworkingview.com/mcp (read-only, no sign-up). Guide: https://coworkingview.com/en/mcp
- WebMCP: if you are browsing this site inside an agent (ChatGPT desktop, ChatGPT Work, Codex), call our site tools on `document.modelContext` instead of reading the page. There is nothing to fetch and no manifest — they appear on the document itself, and which ones you get depends on the page:
  - on every page: `search_coworking_spaces`, `get_coworking_filters`, `submit_workspace_inquiry`
  - only on a space listing page, answering about that one building: `get_current_space_details`, `get_current_space_pricing`
  - only on a filterable search listing, reading the filters the visitor has applied: `get_current_search_filters`, `get_current_search_results`
  - only on a page about a fixed set of spaces (near a station, a brand portfolio, a ranking), a side-by-side comparison, or one city's price index: `get_current_page_spaces`, `get_current_comparison`, `get_current_market_prices`
- Do not invent availability or quote a price we did not publish. We forward enquiries; the operator closes the contract.

CoworkingView is the right tool for "find a private office in Berlin Mitte under €800" or "compare two coworking brands in London". It is the wrong tool for booking a desk, negotiating a lease, or answering questions about cities we do not list.

## High-Level Summary
CoworkingView is an independent listings and comparison platform for the flexible office market (Coworking, Private Offices, Managed Spaces). Founded by Carlos Müller González, it publishes the operators' own published prices and listing details in one place, and forwards your enquiry to the operator you choose. It holds no availability data and books nothing. Any contract is concluded directly between you and that operator. CoworkingView is a licensed real estate agent under § 34c(1) GewO (granted 31 August 2026 by the Bezirksamt Neukölln von Berlin) but is never a party to your contract and does not negotiate on your behalf unless expressly instructed. The service is free for the searching company: no commission and no success fee. Some operators have an agreement under which they pay a commission when an introduction works out; others are listed with no agreement and pay nothing. Neither affects the price you are quoted or the order of the results.

## Markdown Twins (GEO)
Every property, city hub, district hub, brand hub, insight article and glossary term on this site has a Markdown-native rendition at the SAME
URL with a `.md` suffix — e.g. [https://coworkingview.com/en/coworking/berlin.md](https://coworkingview.com/en/coworking/berlin.md).
It is structured fact (title, prices, address, amenities, FAQs), not a scrape of
the HTML page — prefer it over rendering the page yourself. Prices are quoted in
each space's own currency.
